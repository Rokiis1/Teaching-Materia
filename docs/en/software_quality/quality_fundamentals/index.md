**Quality Fundamentals** establishes the conceptual foundation for understanding and evaluating software quality. Building on the [Software Quality introduction](../index.md), this area introduces the fundamental concepts used to describe and assess software quality.

## Scope of Quality Fundamentals

The area examines distinctions between errors, defects, and failures, as well as between prevention and detection, quality assurance and quality control, and verification and validation. It also introduces how requirements, evidence, and quality models contribute to understanding software quality. The emphasis is on the meaning and relationships of these concepts rather than the detailed procedures used to apply them.

These foundations support [Software Testing](../software_testing/index.md), [Quality Engineering](../quality_engineering/index.md), [Quality Management](../quality_management/index.md), and [Quality Attributes & Evaluation](../quality_attributes_and_evaluation/index.md). Each area applies the shared concepts through its own methods and practices. To begin studying these concepts, start with the [Fundamentals of Software Quality](fundamentals_of_software_quality/overview.md) module.

The terminology and principles discussed here draw on established software engineering literature, standards, and professional guidance, as documented in the following references.
